Arabian Horse

Other names: Arabian, Arab

Country of origin: Developed in the Middle East, most notably Arabian Peninsula

Distribution: Worldwide from Middle East and North Africa origins

Traits:

  • Weight: 800 to 1,000 lb (360 to 450 kg)
  • Height: 14.1 to 15.1 hands (57 to 61 inches, 145 to 155 cm)
  • Color: Bay, black, chestnut, or gray. Occasional dominant white, sabino, or rabicano patterns.
  • Distinguishing features: Finely chiseled bone structure, concave profile, arched neck, comparatively level croup, high-carried tail.

Akhal-Teke

Country of origin: Turkmenistan

Traits:

  • Weight: 430–500 kg (950–1100 lb)
  • Height: 144–160 cm (14.1–15.3 h)
  • Distinguishing features: Riding horse bred for endurance; noted for 'metallic' coat of some individuals.

Friesian Horse

Country of origin: Netherlands

Traits:

  • Height: 15 to 16 hands (60 to 64 inches, 152 to 163 cm)
  • Color: Black
  • Distinguishing features: Black, well muscled, thick mane and tail, feather on lower legs, and high action.

Kazakh Purebred

Other names: Kazakh

Country of origin: Kazakhstan

Traits:

  • Distinguishing features: Easy keeper, great endurance and stamina. The Kazakh horse averages 144 cm (14.0+1⁄2 hands; 56+1⁄2 in) for stallions and mares average 142 cm (14.0 hands; 56 in). They weigh between 400 and 500 kg (880 and 1,100 lb). The breed is criticized for a short stride and a jolting trot. However, they are also very hardy and able to cover long distances.

Shire Horse

Country of origin: United Kingdom

Distribution: United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, United States

Use: Draught, show

Traits:

  • Weight: Stallions 900–1100 kg, Geldings 850–1100 kg
  • Height: Stallion 173 cm (17.0 hands) minimum, Gelding 168 cm (16.2 h) minimum, Mare 163 cm (16.0 h) minimum
  • Color: Bay, black, brown or grey
  • Distinguishing features: Large size, draught conformation, feathered legs

Russian Heavy Draft Horse

Other names: Russkii Tyazhelovoz

Traits:

  • Weight: 580–700 kg (650 kg average for males, 575 kg average for females)
  • Height: 144–152 cm (152 cm average for males, 149 cm average for females)
